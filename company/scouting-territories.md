# Scouting Territories — LocalBoost

One-time research, compiled 2026-10-09. Ranked by: independent (non-chain)
restaurant density × visible signs of weak online presence × accessibility for
outreach. Scout must verify every individual claim live before logging or
contacting — territory notes are starting points, not facts about specific
restaurants.

---

## 1. Oak Tree Road, Edison / Iselin (Middlesex County)

**Streets:** Oak Tree Rd, roughly the 1600–2000 blocks (Edison into Iselin).
**Cuisine mix:** Overwhelmingly Indian — North Indian/Mughlai, South Indian,
Gujarati thali houses, Indo-Chinese, biryani corners, sweet shops. Dozens of
family-run spots (Moghul Express, Bombay Blue, Desi Dhamaka, Kathiyawadi
Kitchen, Gordhan Thal, Bawarchi Biryani Corner among many).
**Why it's the best hunting:** The densest concentration of independent
restaurants in the coverage area, and many have visibly dated or bare-bones
websites (several are simple template sites). Owners are typically hands-on
families focused on the kitchen, not marketing. Bonus: it's the user's home
cuisine territory (Eggholic connection) — the pitch lands naturally, and
walk-in visits are easy.

## 2. India Square, Jersey City (Journal Square area)

**Streets:** Newark Ave between Kennedy Blvd and the Pulaski Skyway.
**Cuisine mix:** South Asian — Indian, Bangladeshi, Pakistani restaurants,
sweet counters, spice markets; more than a dozen eateries (e.g. Rasoi at
810 Newark Ave, Honest Restaurant at 811 Newark Ave).
**Why it's great hunting:** Local coverage describes this stretch as still
needing revitalization and lacking downtown's polish and foot traffic —
exactly the profile of restaurants being overlooked online. Family-run,
older establishments, strong community roots, minimal marketing
sophistication. Dense enough to work an entire afternoon on foot.

## 3. Central Avenue, Jersey City Heights

**Streets:** Central Ave, roughly 240–400 blocks (07307).
**Cuisine mix:** Old-school Italian delis (Andrea Salumeria), Mexican/
Salvadorian (Irmas), Peruvian (El Gordo), Haitian/West African (Griot Cafe),
Japanese (Ozu Foods), family diners (Tommy's Family Restaurant, Central
Bistro).
**Why it's great hunting:** A classic main street of decades-old independent
businesses — delis, bakeries, and family restaurants that have survived on
regulars, not Google. Very high independent density, five continents of
cuisine in a few blocks (there's even a walking food tour of it), and the
least likely to have modern websites or review management.

## 4. West Side Avenue corridor, Jersey City

**Streets:** West Side Ave, roughly 320–770 blocks; plus nearby JFK Blvd spots.
**Cuisine mix:** Dominican (La Isla), Vietnamese (Thanh Huong, 20 years in
business), Mexican (Primicia), Italian deli (Carmine's — cash-only),
Caribbean/seafood (Morgan Seafood, 26 years in business).
**Why it's great hunting:** Family-run global eateries, several operating
20+ years — longevity without modernization is the tell. A cash-only deli in
2026 almost certainly has no online ordering and minimal web presence.
Less foot traffic than downtown, so owners are reachable and competition
for their attention is low.

## 5. Journal Square core, Jersey City

**Streets:** Summit Ave, Bergen Ave, Sip Ave around the Journal Square PATH.
**Cuisine mix:** Bangladeshi (Korai Kitchen — NYT-recognized, family-run),
Indian street food (Kati Roll Co.), Latin (La Brujeria), classic diners
(Miss America Diner, All New Vip Diner), sandwich shops.
**Why it's good hunting:** A transit-hub neighborhood with affordable rents
sustains a wide mix of independents rather than chains. Several beloved
family-run spots with national-press-worthy food but neighborhood-level
marketing. Good second wave after India Square, and walkable from it.

## Honorable mention (lower priority)

**Downtown / Newark Ave Restaurant Row (Grove St to Jersey Ave):** Very dense
but trendier and more polished — these restaurants tend to have decent
websites and active Instagrams already. Worth a pass later, not first.

## Suggested scouting order for Scout

Week 1: Oak Tree Road (Edison/Iselin) → Week 2: India Square + Journal Square
core (one trip) → Week 3: Central Ave (Heights) → Week 4: West Side Ave.
Re-score and rotate based on reply rates.
